Why Study in Canada?

Canada has rapidly emerged as one of the world's most sought-after study destinations, offering affordable world-class education combined with some of the most welcoming immigration policies globally. With top-ranked universities such as the University of Toronto, UBC, and McGill, Canadian degrees are highly valued by employers worldwide.

What truly sets Canada apart is the Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP), which allows graduates to work in Canada for up to 3 years after completing their studies. This serves as a direct pathway to permanent residency through the Express Entry system. Canada is also one of the safest countries in the world, with a multicultural society, high quality of life, and co-op programmes that provide paid work experience during your studies.
