Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common queries about studying abroad

Is the initial counselling really free?

Yes, absolutely. Your first counselling session is completely free with no obligation. We assess your profile, discuss your goals, and give you an honest roadmap before you commit to anything.

How long does the entire process take from start to departure?

Typically 3–6 months depending on your destination and intake. We recommend starting at least 6–8 months before your desired intake for the best university options and scholarship opportunities.

Do you help with education loans and scholarships?

Yes. We assist with education loan applications through our banking partners and help identify scholarship opportunities that match your academic profile. Our financial planning support is part of our end-to-end service.

What exams do I need to take, and do you offer coaching?

Requirements vary by country and programme. Common exams include IELTS, TOEFL, GRE, and GMAT. We offer in-house coaching for all major standardised tests as well as language courses in German, French, and Japanese.

Can I study abroad on a budget?

Definitely. Countries like Germany offer low or zero tuition at public universities. We also help identify affordable options in Canada, Ireland, and New Zealand, plus assist with scholarships and part-time work opportunities.
